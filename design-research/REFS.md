# REFS - Undertow shock console

Research pass for a deep-water dark DeFi risk console: pick an asset, apply a haircut, watch the loss travel through wrappers and vault-of-vaults. Captured with `refshot.py` at 1440x900 and 390x844. North star = Hyperliquid (terminal density, semantic loss colour). Secondaries = Spark (ranked left rail + inline stat strip) and Morpho Vaults (the domain's own product, the screen ours replaces). Domain refs: Hyperliquid, Spark, Curve, Uniswap, Silo, Morpho vaults, Morpho modal. Outside-domain refs (by problem shape: "a graph that propagates", "a ranked queue with a context panel", "editorial numbers with provenance"): d3 force graph, TradingView, Our World in Data, Kumu.

## Hyperliquid trade - terminal density, semantic loss colour (NORTH STAR)
Perp terminal: a top price strip in tabular figures, a zoned body (chart / book / entry), a right rail of watchlist rows. The move worth stealing: **a single monospace-ish numeral style for every figure, fixed-width, right-aligned**, so `2,420.98` and a loss column read as one grid; body text `OurFont 16px w400 lh normal`, data rows `OurFont 12px`, muted teal `rgb(151,252,228)` links, radius `8px` on buttons only. Red/green are used strictly for change, never decoration. Numbers: every row is "symbol + last + chg + chg%", the last two coloured. Primary action is a single filled CTA in the header. What NOT to take: the sheer count of controls (dozens of toolbar icons) and the 4-pane chrome; our console is one job. Empty state: the terminal never shows one, it shows a picker. Paths: `refs/hyperliquid-trade-1440.png`, `refs/hyperliquid-trade-390.png`.

## Spark - ranked left rail, inline stat strip, accented figure
Lending/savings product on near-black `rgb(245,245,250)`-on-dark. The move worth stealing: **a vertical list of selectable rows where the name sits left and the number sits right**, the selected row carrying an accent border and soft glow; and a one-line stat strip above the panel where each stat is muted-label + bold-value inline (`TVL $300.1M | Users 1K | Liquidity 4.2B USDC`). Type: `Roobert 48px w500 tr-0.48px lh60px` headline, `Roobert 24px tr-0.24px` section, `Inter var 16px` body, buttons `r8px tr-0.08px`. One accent (pink) with one job (the primary action), and a tabbed panel (`Savings Rate / Collateral Composition / Liquidity / Risk Rating`) for secondary detail. What NOT to take: the decorative thin-line background arcs (noise), and the giant marketing headline above a product table. Empty state not shown. Paths: `refs/spark-1440.png`, `refs/spark-390.png`.

## Morpho Vaults - the domain's own product (the screen ours replaces)
Dark DeFi lending UI, `FK Grotesk 20px w500` section heads over `Inter Variable 16px w450` body, `rgba(255,255,255,0.95)` text, small `r8px` pills at `13px`. The move worth stealing: **restrained monochrome with a single functional accent and 8px radii**, long dense rows separated by hairlines rather than cards-in-cards, and label/value pairs where the value is the louder element. Numbers are right-aligned with tabular figures and a muted unit suffix; percentages carry a colour ONLY when they mean risk. Primary action is one filled button per row. What NOT to take: everything behind the terms modal on the parent route (see below), and the "wrapped by" chip spam when overused. Empty state: a plain centered sentence, no illustration. Paths: `refs/morpho-vaults-1440.png`, `refs/morpho-vaults-390.png`.

## Morpho terms modal - the anti-pattern to avoid (domain, negative-leaning)
Loading `app.morpho.org` cold returns a full-viewport legal consent modal before any product pixel. Measured: a dimmed scrim over the app, `FK Grotesk 38px w400 lh44px` heading over `Inter Variable 16px w450` legal copy, a scroll box, and a single disabled-until-scrolled primary button. The lesson is structural, not visual: **a wall in front of the first screen loses the cold reader.** Our console must show the working surface (asset picker + empty-state line) with no gate. Keep the visual calm; reject the interruption. What NOT to take: the modal-as-homepage, the scrim, and a CTA that is disabled by a scroll position. Paths: `refs/morpho-1440.png`.

## Curve - top-bar stat strip, dark DEX chrome
Dark exchange chrome on `#000`-ish, `Mona Sans 16px w400` body, `14px w600` buttons at `r0px` (hard corners), an orange warning banner as the only loud element. The move worth stealing: **the inline stat strip in the header** - `Total Deposits $1.27b  Daily Volume $38.92m  Crypto Volume Share 14.34%` - muted labels and near-white values on one baseline, tabular figures, no boxes. Also: hard-coded corners on chrome, radius reserved for the one modal card. Numbers: always units attached, never a bare float. Primary action is a single full-width bar in the card. What NOT to take: the `r0px` everywhere (too austere for a long session) and the promo/retirement banners. Empty state: form shows `0.00` placeholders and `-` for derived rows. Paths: `refs/curve-1440.png`, `refs/curve-390.png`.

## Uniswap - near-black surface, one composed card
Marketing-plus-app shell on `#0d0d0d`. Type `Basel 64px w485 lh76px` display, `36px` / `24px` at 0.38 and 0.65 opacity for a clear three-level hierarchy, one hot accent `rgb(255,55,199)`. The move worth stealing: **a single centered task card on a near-black field**, the card a barely-lighter panel with a token-selector row top and bottom and a swap glyph between them; everything else in the frame is much lower contrast so the card wins. Numbers: token amounts in a large input, USD values small and muted beneath. Primary action: one filled button spanning the card. What NOT to take: the accent hue is banned territory for us, and the lower marketing grid (three equal feature cards) is exactly our slop list. Empty state: `0.00` + `-` placeholders. Paths: `refs/uniswap-1440.png`, `refs/uniswap-390.png`.

## Silo - label/value rows and a risk indicator (light contrast ref)
The one light reference, kept deliberately: it shows the same content problem we have in the opposite theme. `dgA 28px w500 tr-0.28px` section heads, `DM Mono 14px` micro-labels, near-black ink `rgb(15,19,26)` on pale. The move worth stealing: **the repeated label-left / value-right row** (`Network` / `Total deposits` / `Collateral` / `Risk Level`) inside a vault card, and a compact **risk indicator made of three bars** (Low = 1 bar, High = 3 bars) instead of a status pill. Pair rows: label muted at `12px` uppercased, value `14px` mono. Primary action: a card headline (`Supply APR`) then a single button. What NOT to take: the light theme itself (our direction is deep-water dark) and the pastel gradient card washes. Paths: `refs/silo-1440.png`, `refs/silo-390.png`.

## d3 force-directed graph - the component we actually ship (outside, by shape)
The canonical force graph: a full-width SVG on white with drag, zoom, and a tick simulation. Type `Inter Variable 16px`, small `r4px` controls. The move worth stealing: **legibility rules for a graph** - nodes sized by degree, an always-on label with a white halo behind text so it stays readable over edges, directionless links at low opacity, and a `npm install d3` + copy-the-code block so the reader can take the exact code. Measured: default link stroke ~`1.5px`, label ~`10-12px`, node radius scaled by `sqrt(degree)`. What NOT to take: the white canvas, the `0.7` link opacity as a fixed value (we drive opacity/width by loss usd), and the unlabelled example data. Empty state: the sim runs with whatever nodes exist. Paths: `refs/d3-force-1440.png`, `refs/d3-force-390.png`.

## TradingView - dense dark terminal, tabular numbers, semantic colour (outside)
Candlestick terminal on `#0f0f0f`-adjacent surfaces: header OHLC line in mono, a right watchlist of `symbol / last / chg / chg%`, red and green strictly for direction. Type falls back to system `14px` for rows with a compact `12px` for the watchlist. The move worth stealing: **the quote header as a single dense line of label/value pairs** (`O H L C +chg (+%)`) with the same tabular grid as the table below it, and a two-level density - loud price, quiet everything else. Numbers always carry sign and unit and are right-aligned. Primary action: a low-emphasis `Trade` button, top right. What NOT to take: the icon-saturated left toolbar and the neon-teal `rgb(151,252,228)`-style accents; also the fake-impact sparkline habit (we show a real loss number, not a wiggle). Empty state: dashed-axis skeleton. Paths: `refs/tradingview-1440.png`, `refs/tradingview-390.png`.

## Our World in Data - numbers with provenance, editorial calm (outside, by shape)
Data-journalism home: `Playfair Display 32px w600` serif headline over `Lato 16px w400 lh25.6px` body, one warm accent `rgb(247,192,32)`. The move worth stealing: **every figure carries its source and date in small muted type directly beneath it** ("Data source: ... / Our World in Data" in `~12px`), and the chart card pairs a one-sentence takeaway with the plot, so a number is never naked. Also: `Latest` / `Data` / `About` nav where the current section is a solid red block, and a soft warm-grey page field rather than pure white. What NOT to take: the serif display face (our data console is sans + mono) and the very tall editorial scroll. Empty state: charts render axes with no series. Paths: `refs/owid-1440.png`, `refs/owid-390.png`.

## Kumu - network mapping product, graph legibility at product scale (outside)
A network-mapping tool on a pale field with `proxima-nova` UI and `freight-text-pro` headings. The move worth stealing: **the graph-as-canvas with a persistent side rail that filters and a legend that maps colour/shape to meaning**, plus a selection that lights one node's neighbourhood and dims the rest. Our console steals exactly that: click a vault, the path to the shocked asset stays lit, the rest drops to ~20 percent. Numbers in the rail are muted label + value pairs, not big metrics. Primary action is one `Explore` CTA, everything else is navigation. What NOT to take: the marketing chrome, the multi-font mix, and the pale theme (we are dark). Empty state: an empty canvas with a "start a project" prompt. Paths: `refs/kumu-390.png`.

## Boolean check for the Design Read
- North star (Hyperliquid): dense dark terminal, one accent, semantic loss colour, tabular figures -> locked as our skeleton.
- Secondaries (Spark, Morpho Vaults): ranked left rail with accent-bordered selection; monochrome + 8px radii + hairline rows.
- Outside refs (d3, TradingView, OWID, Kumu): graph legibility rules, tabular number grid, provenance line under every figure, neighbourhood spotlight on selection.
- Refusal list, stolen from the negatives above: no terms-modal wall, no three-equal-feature-card grid, no icon strip, no sparkline theatre, no serif display face, no pastel gradient card washes, no status-pill spam.
